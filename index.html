About the gym

Serious training, right in Mainpura.

Titan Pro Gym is a newly opened strength and fitness gym on Gosain Tola Road, Mainpura. The floor is kitted out with well-maintained machines and free weights, and the training is led by specialised coaches who build your plan around your goals — not a one-size-fits-all routine.

Members tell us the same things again and again: proper guidance on form, a spacious and hygienic floor that never feels overcrowded, and staff who actually know your name. That is the kind of place we set out to run.

  • Customised workout plans and hands-on form coaching
  • Spacious, hygienic floor with modern machines and free weights
  • Genuine supplements available on-site
  • Friendly team and a motivating crowd
